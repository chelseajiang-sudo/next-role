Email Setup

Connected to Microsoft Outlook
This dashboard opens emails directly in your Outlook app. No configuration needed — it uses your system default mail application.
How it works: 1. Fill in recipients, subject, and body in the Compose window.
2. Drag any files from the Documents panel into the Attachments zone.
3. Click Open in Outlook — Outlook opens with everything pre-filled.
4. Attached files are automatically downloaded to your Downloads folder.
5. Drag the downloaded files into the Outlook window and hit Send.
Tip: Make sure Outlook (desktop or web) is set as your system's default email app for the best experience. On Windows: Settings → Apps → Default apps → Mail.
